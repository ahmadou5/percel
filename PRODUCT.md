# PRODUCT.md — Percel

## Product Purpose
Percel is a Nigerian logistics platform: two-sided waybill delivery (customers + drivers) with an embedded wallet. Three surfaces share one brand:

1. **apps/web** — public marketing site + public parcel tracking + APK download. First touch for customers and businesses evaluating Percel.
2. **apps/admin** — internal control tower for operators: orders, live fleet map, driver KYC review, payouts/cashouts, disputes, support tickets, settings.
3. **apps/user / apps/driver** — Expo mobile apps (out of scope for most design work here).

## Users
- **web:** Nigerian SMEs and individuals shipping parcels interstate/intrastate; also drivers checking out the platform before downloading the app. Often on mid-range Android phones, sometimes slow connections.
- **admin:** Percel's own ops team (support, finance, dispatch). Power users, sitting in front of large screens all day, processing money and disputes under time pressure. Trust in every number on screen is critical.

## Brand & Tone (from brand.md)
Premium, operational, precise. "Control tower": deep navy surfaces, high contrast, cobalt primary (#5B8CFF), restrained amber accents, Space Grotesk + IBM Plex Mono. Voice: concise, operational, confident, no fluff.

For **web** the same brand should feel warmer and more inviting without losing precision: this is logistics you can trust with your goods, not crypto hype. Anti-references: neon-on-black crypto aesthetics, generic SaaS landing pages with identical card grids, fake dashboard screenshots, stock-photo people in warehouses.

## Register
- `apps/admin` → **product** (design serves the work; calm data density, sharp hierarchy)
- `apps/web` → **brand** (design IS the product; must earn trust and convert downloads)

## Strategic Principles
1. Money is never decorative: balances, refunds, payout states must be unambiguous.
2. Real data beats mock polish: no fabricated numbers anywhere.
3. Speed of comprehension over decoration: operators scan, they don't admire.
4. Nigerian reality first: Naira formatting, state/hub geography, low-bandwidth tolerance.
