# Brand - Percel Admin

_Status: active_

Percel Admin is the control tower for a logistics platform. The brand should feel premium, operational, and precise: deep surfaces, high-contrast typography, cobalt primary actions, and restrained amber accents for highlights and warnings.

## Palette

- Background: `#07111D`
- Elevated surface: `#0D1728`
- Card surface: `#101B2E`
- Border: `#1D2A44`
- Foreground: `#EAF0FF`
- Muted foreground: `#8FA2C7`
- Primary: `#5B8CFF`
- Primary soft: `#C7D5FF`
- Accent: `#F0B35A`
- Success: `#2DD4BF`
- Warning: `#F7B955`
- Destructive: `#FB7185`

## Typography

- Sans: `Space Grotesk`
- Mono: `IBM Plex Mono`

## Voice

- Concise
- Operational
- Confident
- No fluff

## Feel

- Premium infrastructure dashboard
- Calm data density
- Sharp hierarchy
- Subtle gradients, never loud color noise
